# Gravity Simulator Blog

## 🌍🌕 Simulating Gravity: Earth vs. Moon & Newton's Cradle

This blog explores the physics behind gravity simulations, comparing Earth's and the Moon's gravitational effects, and demonstrating Newton’s Cradle using accurate physics models.

---

## 🔬 Physics Behind the Simulations

### 🌍 Earth vs. 🌕 Moon Gravity
- **Earth’s Gravity**: 9.81 m/s²
- **Moon’s Gravity**: 1.62 m/s² (~1/6th of Earth's)
- **Effect**: Objects fall slower on the Moon, leading to different motion dynamics in our simulator.

### ⚖️ Newton's Cradle Simulation
- Demonstrates **momentum conservation** & **energy transfer**.
- Realistic collision modeling ensures accurate physics representation.
- Accounts for **elasticity, damping, and friction**.
